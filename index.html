My Journey & Philosophy

I grew up in Middlesbrough, in Teesside, and my early life was shaped by trauma and addiction – experiences that deeply affected the choices open to me at the time. Through that darkness, I was fortunate to receive the support, care, and guidance I needed to rebuild my life. That transformation gave me a deep desire to give back and to dedicate myself to helping others find their own path to healing.

For more than 20 years I have worked in the mental health field. I began in residential care, where I spent many years supporting people through severe substance misuse and recovery from addiction. Moving into broader community care, I expanded my practice to support people with a wide range of mental health challenges, including trauma, depression, severe anxiety, and life transitions.

Today I am self-employed, and my work includes counselling NHS patients, offering accessible, compassionate, and empowering therapeutic support. My approach combines lived experience with 19 years of clinical practice, giving you a safe, non-judgemental space where you are genuinely heard and respected, and supported to make lasting positive change. I work in a person-centred way, drawing on rational emotive behaviour therapy (REBT), cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), transactional analysis (TA) and motivational interviewing.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if counselling is right for me?
If you are feeling overwhelmed, stuck, or anxious, or are facing difficult emotional circumstances, counselling offers a confidential space to work through your thoughts and find useful ways of coping.
How do group therapy sessions work?
Groups cost £30 per person for a two-hour session (groups of around 8). Groups are arranged on request, so how often a group meets, and where, is agreed with the group. Smaller groups can be discussed. I guide each session carefully.
What happens in the first session?
Your first session is shorter, 40 minutes for £30. We talk about what brings you to counselling and what you hope to achieve, and make sure my approach feels comfortable and right for you.
