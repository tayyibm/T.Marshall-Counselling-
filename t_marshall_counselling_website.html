My Journey & Philosophy

Growing up in the Teesside area of Middlesbrough, my early life was shaped by trauma and addiction—experiences that deeply impacted the decisions and directions available to me at the time. Through that darkness, I was fortunate to receive the support, care, and guidance necessary to rebuild my life. That transformation lit a profound desire in me to give back and dedicate my life to helping others find their path to healing.

For around 20 years now, I have worked continuously in the counselling field. My journey began in residential care, where I spent many years supporting individuals working through severe substance abuse and addiction recovery. Transitioning into broader community care, I expanded my practice to support people navigating a wide spectrum of mental health challenges, including trauma, depression, severe anxiety, and life transitions.

Today, I work alongside the NHS and local community organizations, providing accessible, compassionate, and empowering therapeutic support. My approach combines lived empathy with 20 years of clinical practice, providing you with a safe, non-judgemental environment where you are genuinely heard, respected, and empowered to create lasting positive change.
