Privacy notice

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What happens to your information when you use this website or send a message through it.

Who is responsible for your information

I am Terry Marshall, trading as T.Marshall Counselling, and I am responsible for your information (the law calls this the “data controller”).

  • Email: contact@tmarshallcounselling.co.uk
  • Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) registration: [[CONFIRM: ICO registration number — Terry is registering]]

What the contact form sends me

  • your name and email address
  • your phone number, if you give one
  • the service you are asking about, and your message
  • the time you sent it, and a note that you ticked the consent box
  • a self-check result (your total score and your answers), but only if you chose to add one

Self-check results

The PHQ-9 and GAD-7 self-checks are worked out in your own browser. Nothing is sent unless you press “Add this result to my message” and then send the form. What is then sent is your total score, its range and your answer to each question, including (for the PHQ-9) your answer to the question about thoughts of self-harm. Messages may not be read for up to a week, so adding a result is not a way to get help quickly.

A result is health information, so I receive it only with your explicit consent, given by adding it and ticking the box. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing me, and I will delete the result.

How I use your information, and why

To reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to arrange sessions. If you add a self-check result and go on to become a client, I may also use it to help plan our work together and to keep you safe. My reasons in law (the “lawful basis”) are:

  • Contact details and message: you asked me to reply, as a first step towards working together.
  • Anything about your health (a self-check result you chose to add, or anything you choose to tell me in your message): your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the consent box on the contact form.
  • Technical records: my legitimate interest in keeping this website working and safe.

Where your message goes

Your message travels over an encrypted connection to this website, which emails it straight to my mailbox on the same server, in Germany (the hosting company is Hetzner Online GmbH). No other company’s form, analytics or advertising service is involved, and the contact form itself stores no copy. The hosting company’s security systems may log details of a request they block.

If we meet by video, the call takes place over a service such as Microsoft Teams, Google Meet or WhatsApp, and that company’s own privacy notice covers the call.

Email is never perfectly secure, so please keep your first message brief: a few lines and the times that suit you are plenty. We can talk about anything more personal when we speak.

No cookies, nothing from other companies

This website sets no cookies, has no analytics or advertising tools, and loads nothing (not even fonts or pictures) from other companies. If you follow a link to another website, such as the ICO’s, that website’s own privacy notice applies.

Technical records

Like almost every website, this one keeps a standard record of visits on its server (an “access log”), with details such as your IP address (the number your internet connection uses to reach websites), the time and the page you asked for. It is used only to keep the website running and secure, and is kept by the hosting company for three months.

When you send the form, a one-way scrambled version of your IP address (not the address itself) is kept for at most 24 hours, only to stop the form being flooded with spam. Your IP address is not included in the email.

How long I keep enquiries

If you do not go on to become a client, I keep your enquiry for up to 12 months and then delete it. You can ask me to delete it sooner.

Confidentiality

What you share with me is treated as confidential, but there are limits. I may need to share information without your consent if I believe you or someone else is at serious risk of harm, if there is a concern about the safety of a child or a vulnerable adult, or if the law requires it (for example, a court order). Wherever I can, I will talk to you first. Like all BACP members, I discuss my work in confidential supervision, without identifying you.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of the information I hold about you
  • correct it or delete it
  • stop using it, or limit how I use it

To do any of these, or to withdraw your consent, email contact@tmarshallcounselling.co.uk. I will reply within one month, and explain if I cannot do what you ask.

If you are unhappy

I would welcome the chance to put things right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
